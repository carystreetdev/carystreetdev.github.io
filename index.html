Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No account to create. The developer never receives your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • Online leaderboards are run by Google (Android) and Apple (iPhone), and only if you sign in to them.

This policy covers the Cary Street app on Google Play and the App Store. It explains what information the game uses and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your high scores, the initials you enter for them, your points, the levels and vehicles you've unlocked, and your settings. This save file stays on your device. It is never sent to the developer, and uninstalling the game deletes it. Settings > RESET PROGRESS also clears it.

Online leaderboards

The game can post your run scores to online leaderboards:

  • Android: Google Play Games. If you're signed in to Play Games, your scores are sent to Google and shown with your Play Games profile name and picture. Google's privacy policy covers this.
  • iPhone: Game Center. If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple and shown with your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy covers this.

The developer can see these leaderboards (scores and profile names) the same way other players can, and nothing more about you. Signing out of Play Games or Game Center stops your scores from being posted. You can remove your leaderboard data through your Google or Apple account settings.

Purchases (iPhone)

On iPhone you can choose to pay $3 to support the developer. It unlocks nothing. Apple handles the payment. The developer never sees your payment details or your Apple ID, only Apple's sales totals and a confirmation in the game that the purchase went through. The Android version has nothing to buy.

What the game doesn't do

  • Show ads, or use your device's advertising ID.
  • Use analytics, crash reporting or tracking services.
  • Ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

Cary Street is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects none from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts using information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date at the top.

Common questions

How do I get my progress onto a new phone? Progress is saved on the phone itself, so it doesn't move to a new one. Leaderboard scores stay with your Play Games or Game Center account.

I paid to support the game. Did I get anything? Thank you! The purchase is a tip and unlocks nothing. The whole game is free for everyone.

Where's the tutorial? Settings > SHOW TUTORIAL (or TUTORIAL on iPhone).
